// 03 — how it works

Process

Four steps, no surprises. You always know what's happening and what comes next.

  1. 01

    Brief

    You tell me what you need. I ask the right questions and we agree on scope, timeline and deliverables before any code is written.

  2. 02

    Build

    I build in small, working increments and share progress as I go, so there's never anything to guess about.

  3. 03

    Review

    You test a working version. We refine it together until it does exactly what you need.

  4. 04

    Deliver

    Final handover: source code, deployment and a short walkthrough, so you're never stuck afterwards.
